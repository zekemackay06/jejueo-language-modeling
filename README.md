# Language Modeling in Jejueo

Final project for LINGUIS 373 (Computational Linguistics). I implement several methods of language modeling in the low-resource language Jejueo. 

## What is Jejueo?

Jejueo is the language of Jeju Island, off the coast of South Korea. It is the only language related to Korean that is still spoken anywhere in the world. It was historically classified as a dialect of Korean, and institutions in South Korea still refer to it as such. However, it is now agreed upon in academia to be a separate language from Korean, and the two are sparsely mutually intelligible. Today, Jejueo is considered critically endangered and the Jeju province takes great efforts to support its revitalization.
